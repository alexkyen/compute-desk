Procurement implications

Compute figures are not directly comparable across precision and sparsity. Hopper's displayed tensor figure uses FP8; Blackwell's and Rubin's use FP4. Benchmark the target model and serving or training stack before converting the displayed specification into a performance or price premium.

Roadmap cadence belongs in the contract model. A multi-year capacity term may overlap successor launches. Incumbent pricing still depends on supply, workload demand, provider financing, and successor availability, so address refresh, substitution, and rate-review rights explicitly.

Facility readiness becomes part of product fit at rack scale. Higher rack power density and liquid cooling requirements narrow the set of deployable facilities. Request delivered power, cooling, commissioning, and acceptance milestones alongside accelerator availability.
