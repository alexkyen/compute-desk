Buying one? Here’s what to check.

The scale-up domain ends at the rack. The NVLink fabric provides each GPU high-bandwidth access across a 72-GPU domain while each GPU retains its own HBM. Traffic leaving that domain moves to the scale-out network, whose media, topology, and performance are procured separately; the network-tax page models that boundary.

Power is the binding constraint. Roughly 120 kW in a single rack is why liquid cooling is a requirement rather than an option, and why the binding constraint in procurement migrated from chip allocation to facility qualification. Two suppliers quoting the same GPU can differ entirely in whether their building can actually run this object.

The rack can be a commercial unit, but it is not the only one. Providers may sell full racks, nodes, instances, or capacity blocks. Ask which physical assets, failure domains, and acceptance criteria correspond to the quoted unit.

Ask the supplierIs the facility NVL72-qualified—power density and liquid loops delivered, not planned—and what physical unit, failure domain, and acceptance test sit behind the quote?
