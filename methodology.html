How to read the evidence labels
LIST
A public provider rate card or official specification. Configuration, region, term, and verification date belong with the value.
MKT
A dated marketplace observation. It does not guarantee available capacity.
DEAL
A value reported in a filing, earnings call, customer announcement, or attributable press report. It may not compare directly with list prices.
EST
An author calculation or range derived from adjacent public evidence. The atlas includes these by default; use “Linked sources only” to restrict the board to observations with a source link.
MODEL
An illustrative scenario output. Its assumptions are part of the result.
What a “GPU-hour” price includes

Prices are shown in US dollars per GPU-hour. An instance price is divided by the number of GPUs in that instance. Unless a source note says otherwise, values exclude tax, storage, support, egress, and data-transfer charges.

The chip’s form factor, host CPU and memory, network, region, availability, and commitment terms can make two GPU-hours very different products. Use the atlas to ask better questions about a quote; its points are not offers you can execute.

Where the models stop
Capacity planning ↗

The synthetic example assumes a stationary demand distribution. Imported hourly demand is sorted: it preserves how long demand lasts, not when it occurs. Neither mode forecasts growth, correlated outages, contract minimums, or counterparty risk.

Network performance ↗

The model estimates one ring all-reduce communication component, combined with your compute-time and overlap assumptions. It does not reproduce application-level goodput, congestion, sharding, storage, or every NCCL optimization. Use measured NCCL bandwidth when available.

Network layouts ↗

Switch and link counts are planning estimates under the displayed endpoint, switch-radix, rail, oversubscription, and redundancy assumptions. A vendor design can allocate ports and redundant planes differently.

Contract scenarios ↗

Dollar values are scenario exposure, not probability-adjusted expected value or additive savings. Clause examples are a composite negotiation aid, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel.

The 3D models ↗

The rack and row show relationships, not construction drawings. Cable counts, placement, and some geometry are simplified for legibility. Confirm the actual hardware and facility design with the supplier.

Dates, updates, and the July price snapshot

The price atlas is an archived July 2026 snapshot, last verified on 22 July 2026. It is labeled as historical reference throughout the atlas. It is not a current quotation service.

The repository check rejects a live market dataset more than 45 days old. An archived dataset must keep its original verification date and a visible historical-data notice. Relabeling it does not refresh the evidence.

Hardware specifications retain their original July 2026 verification labels. Interactive calculations remain useful for exploring your own assumptions; neither a new interface nor an editorial explanation makes the underlying sources newer.
