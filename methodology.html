Confidence layer

Methods and sources

The Compute Desk is a set of decision aids, not a quotation system, engineering design package, or legal opinion. This page defines what its numbers mean, where the models stop, and how freshness is signaled.

Evidence grades

  • LIST — a public provider rate card or official specification. Configuration, region, term, and verification date should appear with the value.
  • MKT — an observable marketplace or clearing price. It is a time-stamped market observation, not a capacity guarantee.
  • DEAL — a value reported in a filing, earnings call, customer announcement, or attributable press report. It is not necessarily comparable with list pricing.
  • EST — an author calculation or range derived from adjacent public evidence. Estimates are excluded by default when a page offers a confidence filter.
  • MODEL — an illustrative scenario output. It is sensitive to the displayed assumptions and is not a forecast.

Price normalization

Prices are shown in US dollars per GPU-hour. Instance prices are divided by the number of GPUs in the instance. Unless the tooltip states otherwise, values exclude tax, storage, support, egress, and data-transfer charges. Accelerator form factor, host CPU and memory, fabric, region, availability, and commitment mechanics can make two nominally equal GPU-hours non-comparable.

Use: benchmark the band around a quote and identify diligence questions. Do not use: treat a plotted point as an executable offer.

Model boundaries

Supply book

The synthetic mode uses a stationary load-duration curve. The imported-data mode sorts observed hourly demand and therefore preserves duration, not chronology. Neither mode forecasts growth, correlated outages, contract minimums, or counterparty risk.

Network tax

The model estimates a ring all-reduce communication component and combines it with user-supplied compute time and overlap. It does not reproduce topology-aware NCCL tuning, congestion, sharding, pipeline bubbles, storage, or application-level goodput. A measured NCCL bandwidth input should be preferred when available.

Topologies

Equipment counts are planning estimates under the displayed endpoint, switch-radix, rail, oversubscription, and redundancy assumptions. A vendor design can allocate ports and redundant planes differently.

Issues list

Dollar values are scenario exposure, not probability-adjusted expected value and not additive savings. Clause examples are a composite negotiation aid, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel.

Freshness policy

Price observations carry a verification month. The automated repository check warns when the market-data verification date is more than 45 days old. Hardware specifications are rechecked after major vendor announcements. Editorial conclusions do not inherit the authority of their cited sources.
