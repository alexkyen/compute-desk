METHODS & SOURCES

How to interpret the numbers

The desk combines published facts, author estimates, and scenario calculations. Their sources, dates, and boundaries determine what each number can support.

01

Published evidence

A manufacturer specification, a dated rate card, or a reported deal.

Example: 72 GPUs in an NVL72 rack, from the manufacturer’s specification.

EVIDENCE
02

Author estimates

A range inferred from adjacent evidence, with more uncertainty than a directly quoted value.

Example: an estimated price range on the atlas. Keep the estimate label attached.

INFERENCE
03

Model outputs

A calculation under stated assumptions. Its result changes when those assumptions change.

Example: $2 reserved ÷ $6 on demand = 33.3% breakeven utilization.

MODEL

A SIMPLE NORMALIZATION

What does one GPU-hour cost?

Divide the instance price by the number of GPUs included. This makes the billing units comparable; different hardware, networks, and terms can still deliver different value.

HYPOTHETICAL 8-GPU INSTANCE

$24per instance-hour÷8GPUs=$3per GPU-hour

Running continuously for a 730-hour month would cost $17,520 before additional charges. This is an arithmetic example, not a provider quote.

EVIDENCE DATE

The price atlas is historical.

Its archived July 2026 snapshot was last verified on 22 July 2026. It is a reference for comparing market structure, not a current quotation service.

Hardware specifications retain their July 2026 verification labels, with one targeted correction dated 16 October 2026: GB200 GPU memory is 186 GB per GPU, derived from NVIDIA’s 372 GB per two-GPU superchip. The rack page links to that specification. Other hardware entries have not been reverified.

How to read the evidence labels
LIST
A public provider rate card or official specification. Configuration, region, term, and verification date belong with the value.
MKT
A dated marketplace observation. It does not guarantee available capacity.
DEAL
A value reported in a filing, earnings call, customer announcement, or attributable press report. It may not compare directly with list prices.
EST
An author calculation or range derived from adjacent public evidence. The default atlas view shows on-demand observations with source links. Select “All observations” to include estimates; compare the evidence grade before using a value.
MODEL
An illustrative scenario output. Its assumptions are part of the result.
What a “GPU-hour” price includes

Prices are shown in US dollars per GPU-hour. An instance price is divided by the number of GPUs in that instance. Unless a source note says otherwise, values exclude tax, storage, support, egress, and data-transfer charges.

The chip’s form factor, host CPU and memory, network, region, availability, and commitment terms can make two GPU-hours very different products. Use the atlas to ask better questions about a quote; its points are not offers you can execute.

How dates and updates are handled

Archived observations keep their original verification dates and historical-data labels. A new quote requires a new source and verification date; changing a label does not refresh the evidence.

Interactive calculations can be used with your own assumptions. Published hardware and price facts should be checked against the linked source before a purchasing or engineering decision.
