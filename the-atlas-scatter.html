Read the fine print · how to compare fairly

Prices exclude tax, storage, support, egress, and transfer unless a tooltip says otherwise. Region, accelerator form factor, host configuration, fabric, term, and availability can make nominal GPU-hours non-comparable.

Indicative US-market snapshot. No claim of current price or availability.

The logarithmic axis preserves price ratios. A move from $1.50 to $3 occupies the same horizontal distance as a move from $6 to $12. Use the plotted band to identify outliers and negotiation anchors; use the tooltip to recover the exact value, evidence grade, date, and configuration note.

The availability strip distinguishes published, gated, and absent pricing. Each cell represents one accelerator: filled means the seller publishes a price, × means an offering is described but pricing is sales-gated, and blank means no entry was found in the reviewed public materials.

Switch accelerators before ranking sellers. Relative position can change with product, channel, region, term, and availability. Shortlist within the configuration being negotiated rather than treating one product's price rank as a provider-wide conclusion.

What the board leaves out. Published ranges draw as a span from low to high, and the detail behind a gated offering survives in its tooltip. This view is for quote benchmarking, not precision: confirm the region, shape, fabric, term, availability, and ancillary charges with the seller.

Every price carries an evidence label and date. Use the linked-sources filter for a more conservative view, and recheck the underlying page before citing a value in a live negotiation.
