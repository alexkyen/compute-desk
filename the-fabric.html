Fabric diligence

Why the hierarchy matters. Inside one node, GPUs talk over NVLink at hundreds of GB/s. The moment a job outgrows the node, traffic drops onto the network at NIC speed — 400 Gb/s (50 GB/s) per GPU in a standard H100 build — an order of magnitude slower. Training synchronizes gradients across every GPU each step (the all-reduce), so for any model too large for one node, the network sits inside every training step and its bandwidth sets the pace of the whole cluster. The boundary itself is physical: copper NVLink reaches about one rack and no farther — see the rack → — past it, traffic moves onto the optical network, and that is the cost this page measures.

Protocol labels do not establish delivered performance. InfiniBand and RoCE can run the same nominal wire rate and both carry RDMA. Their delivered collective bandwidth depends on NIC and switch generation, topology, congestion control, tuning, and job size. Use line rate only as a ceiling; request measured NCCL bus bandwidth for the proposed configuration.

Ask the supplierInfiniBand or Ethernet, which NIC generation, and what measured NCCL all-reduce bus bandwidth do you see at my job size — not line rate, measured?

Oversubscription changes the value of placement. A 2:1 fabric has half as much uplink capacity out of each leaf as downlink below it. Workloads that remain within a leaf may see little effect; jobs crossing leaf and spine tiers may contend for the reduced uplink capacity. Ask for both the topology diagram and the scheduler's placement policy. The pod page shows the planning ratio physically: compare the tray at 1:1 and 2:1 →

Ask the supplierWhat's the oversubscription ratio, leaf to spine — and can your scheduler place my job within a single leaf or rail?

Rail-optimized designs connect each node NIC to a corresponding switch rail, which can reduce switch count for expected collective patterns. Arbitrary cross-rank and all-to-all traffic may take a less direct path. Validate both the dominant workload and the failure behavior before accepting the cost tradeoff.

Ask the supplierFull fat-tree or rail-optimized? Show me the topology diagram, and tell me the blast radius when one spine switch fails.

Inference requirements vary by serving architecture. Single-node serving may not justify a training-oriented scale-out fabric. Large mixture-of-experts deployments, disaggregated prefill and decode, or models sharded across nodes can create substantial east-west traffic. Size the network from the serving design and measured communication profile, not from the accelerator count alone.

How the proxy is calculated. The model moves one FP16 gradient copy for every parameter through a ring all-reduce, exposes the portion not hidden by overlap, adds that time to the compute-only step, and scales the sticker price by the resulting throughput share. This is a sensitivity calculation, not a benchmark. The 0.92 InfiniBand and 0.80 tuned-RoCE efficiencies are planning assumptions; replace them with measured NCCL bus bandwidth whenever possible.
